Questions, ideas or something not working? Send me an email at my email address (needs JavaScript). I read every message.

Can I listen to music while I train?

Yes. Obertimer's sounds play over your music without stopping it.

What happens if I leave the app during a workout?

The timer keeps time against the clock, so when you come back it shows the right time.

Can I turn off the sounds or the vibration?

Yes. Open Settings in the app and switch off Sounds or Haptics.

Can the screen stay on while I train?

Yes. Turn on Keep screen on in the app's Settings.

Where are my workouts saved?

Only on your phone. If you delete the app, your workouts are deleted too.
