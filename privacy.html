Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

Obertimer does not collect any data. Everything you create in the app stays on your phone.

What the app saves

Your workouts and settings are saved on your device only. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. If you delete the app, they are deleted too.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

There is no sign up. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not connect to the internet.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
